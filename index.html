Celebrating Washoku

Washoku (和食) — Japanese traditional cuisine — is a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. It celebrates seasonal ingredients, natural flavors, and beautiful presentation.

Washoku World brings the joy of Japanese food culture to life through interactive games. Learn the names, appearances, and stories behind beloved dishes.
